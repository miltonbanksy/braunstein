Braunstein Rules

Principles
  • Decision-making, exploration, and role-playing is emphasized.
    • Campaign Setup
    Choose Armor Rules
    Basic Rules
    No Armor If Luck reaches 0, character is out of the fight.
    Move: 50' per round.
    Climb: 10' per round.
    No penalty to stealth, climb, swim, etc.

    Armored Restore 10% of total Luck on KO (round up).
    Move: 30' per round.
    No run, stealth, climb, swim, etc.

    Advanced Rules
    No Armor Move: 50' per round.
    Climb: 10' per round.
    No penalty to stealth, climb, swim, etc.

    Light Armor Restore 10% of total Luck on KO (round up).
    Move: 40' per round.
    Climb: 5' per round. (ruling tbc…)
    -1 penalty to stealth, climb, swim, etc.

    Heavy Armor Restore 20% of total Luck on KO (round up).
    Move: 30' per round.
    No run, stealth, climb, swim, etc.

    Choose Shield Rules
    Basic Rules A shield allows the character to ignore up to 3 points of damage from a single attack, after which the item is damaged and unusable.

    Alternative Rules A shield allows the character to ignore 3 hits of damage incrementally, after which the item is damaged and unusable.

    Adventure Actions
    Spend Luck
  • Spend Luck to survive a hit, trap, hazard, etc.
  • Spend Luck one for one, to increase any dice before or after rolling.
    • Recover Luck
  • Recover Luck by resting. (See 'Rest')
  • Recover Luck for excellent roleplaying.
    • Taking Action
  • Players describe their characters intention and approach.
  • Determine if the action is impossible, requires extra steps, demands a cost, or presents a risk. Players only roll to avoid risks.
  • If the action is risky, determine the difficulty:
    • Easy: Roll 7 or more
    • Moderate: Roll 9 or more
    • Difficult: Roll 12 or more
  • If the action is risky, determine what both success and failure look like.
  • Spend Luck one for one, to increase any dice, before or after rolling.
    • Find Item / Treasure
  • Follow 'Taking Action' procedure
  • On finding an item, determine the value:
    • Common / Small: 1d6 sp, personal items, tools, etc.
    • Exotic / Large: 1d6 x 10 sp, armor, weaponry, etc.
    • Rare / Very Large: 1d6 x 100 sp, luxury item, mount, vehicle, etc.
    • Recall / Recite Lore
  • Any character may spend luck one for one, to increase any dice, before or after rolling.
  • Literate characters benefit from a +1 bonus.
  • Follow 'Taking Action' procedure
    • Repairs
  • Armor 10 sp
  • Shiled 10 sp
    • Rest
  • Camping - Luck +1 per night
  • Inn - Luck +1d6 per night
    • Salvage Armor or Shield
  • 1 in 6 chance of finding a serviceable item
    • Disease
  • Should characters come in contact with a plague carrier and do not otherwise protect themselves, there is a 1-2 in 1d6 chance of contracting the disease and succumbing in 2d6 game days, although any who survive beyond the first week will recover fully.
    • Summon Spirits (Magic users only)
  • This involved conjuring spirits, whether those of the dead and demonic or pagan ones as befits the chronicle and/or period employed.
  • Commanding spirits uses 1d6 LUCK, which also indicates the number of rounds the summoned power lingers before returning from whence it came, whether Hell or the spirit world, etc.
  • These are always non-corporeal and cannot be engaged or otherwise slain, passing through walls and/or moving 50' per round as the summoner demands.
  • Spirits will automatically (no dice required) execute any actions available to a normal character, attacking for 1 hit per round against a single target within 10' and consistently performing at double human intellect and/or physical strength, etc.
    • Divine Offerings
  • The devout can make offerings to their god(s), using animal sacrifice, monetary tithes and confession.
  • Sacrifices should be limited to full moons, holy days, etc.
  • Sacrifices require 1d6 hours of meditation, prayer, ritual, etc.
  • Blessings last one day.
  • Each requires payment in silver, either to procure a suitable animal and/or as a sign of goodwill towards the god and their priesthood:
    • 100 SP Add +1 to all non-combat actions
    • 250 SP Per the above, includes combat
    • 500 SP Per the above, plus Special dispensation. Roll for effect:
      • 1-2: No effect, prior bonuses remain
      • 3-4: Partial success or complication (eg: loss of bonuses, etc.)
      • 5-6: Success with Divine Manifestations beyond expectations!
    • Experience
  • At the end of every playing session, each character is awarded one or more experience points (EXP) as follows:
    • Went on an adventure: 1pt
    • Performed extraordinarily: +1pt
    • Completed a quest: +2pt
  • Experience is cumulative.
  • For every 10 experience points awarded, the character adds 1 LUCK, making them more powerful adventurers and allowing them to better survive in combat and improve their die rolls as well as getting more benefit from armor, etc.
