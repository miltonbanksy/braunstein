Campaign Setup
Choose Armor Rules
Basic Rules
No Armor If Luck reaches 0, character is out of the fight.
Move: 50' per round.
Climb: 10' per round.
No penalty to stealth, climb, swim, etc.

Armored Restore 10% of total Luck on KO (round up).
Move: 30' per round.
No run, stealth, climb, swim, etc.

Advanced Rules
No Armor Move: 50' per round.
Climb: 10' per round.
No penalty to stealth, climb, swim, etc.

Light Armor Restore 10% of total Luck on KO (round up).
Move: 40' per round.
Climb: 5' per round. (ruling tbc…)
-1 penalty to stealth, climb, swim, etc.

Heavy Armor Restore 20% of total Luck on KO (round up).
Move: 30' per round.
No run, stealth, climb, swim, etc.

Choose Shield Rules
Basic Rules A shield allows the character to ignore up to 3 points of damage from a single attack, after which the item is damaged and unusable.

Alternative Rules A shield allows the character to ignore 3 hits of damage incrementally, after which the item is damaged and unusable.
